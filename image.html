These image editing and graphic design projects highlight my passion for visual storytelling and creative expression. SeaShel Logo Design

Designed in Inkscape, this vector logo demonstrates the effective use of typography and visual balance to create a distinctive and professional brand. The project highlights the importance of simplicity and balance in logo design.

Jane Austen's Guide Bookcover

A Regency-inspired book cover created in GIMP that combines vintage-inspired imagery, typography, and color to reflect the beauty and character of the period. The design demonstrates the use of composition and visual hierarchy to create an engaging cover.
