Image Editing

CREATE • EDIT • INSPIRE

The image editing and graphic design projects on this page, highlight my passion for visual storytelling and creative expression. By combining design principles with digital editing techniques, I transformed concepts into compelling and memorable visuals. Each piece reflects the use of color, layout, imagery, and other design principles to create designs that are both aesthetically appealing and purposeful.

SeaShel Logo Design

Designed in Inkscape, this vector logo demonstrates my ability to create clean, scalable graphics using text and graphic elements. I used layers to organize and build each element of the logo. I created the Pearl shell with the Bezier tool, and added the pearl using the shape tool. After refining the design, I grouped the elements and exported the finished logo. The logo can now be used in a variety of digital or print projects. This project strengthened my skills in vector-based design and highlights the importance of simplicity and balance in creating an effective logo design.

Jane Austen's Guide Bookcover

A Regency-inspired book cover created in GIMP that combines vintage-inspired imagery, typography, and color to reflect the beauty and character of the period. The design demonstrates the use of basic design principles such as composition and visual hierarchy to create an engaging cover. I applied image editing techniques such as resizing, layering, filtering, and other effects to combine multiple images into a cohesive and visually appealing design. The final design reflects my ability to use digital imagery effectively to communicate ideas and support learning experiences.

AI-Assisted Book Cover Design

A Regency-inspired book cover created using Canva AI as an assisted creative design tool. This project creates a similar aesthetic of the traditional GIMP book cover through intentional prompting, visual evaluation, and creative judgment, demonstrating the relationship between artificial intelligence and human creativity in digital design.
