CREATIVE STORYTELLING & DIGITAL LEARNING

Video Production

IMAGINE • CREATE • INSPIRE

The video production and editing projects highlight my exploration of visual storytelling in e-learning environments. By combining intentional editing, dynamic transitions, visual effects, and audio elements, I transformed concepts into engaging learning experiences. Each project reflects how multimedia can be used to communicate information effectively while keeping learners motivated and immersed in the content.

Circle B Bar Reserve Educational Video

An educational video created with Microsoft Clipchamp featuring Florida's Circle B Bar Reserve, designed to introduce viewers to the reserve's diverse wildlife and natural beauty. Through intentional editing, transitions, and visual storytelling techniques, the project creates an engaging learning experience that encourages curiosity about Florida's ecosystems.

AI-Assisted Video on Protecting Florida's Reserves

This AI-assisted educational video was created using Google Flow for video generation and ElevenLabs for narration to promote awareness of Florida's natural habitats emphasizing the importance of protecting these ecosystems. This project demonstrates how AI can be integrated into video production to help streamline production while still preserving human creativity, expertise, and judgment. As the creator, it is important that I maintain control over content accuracy and design elements.
